# Dig Anti-Tells

Dig anti-tells are recurring AI-generated UI patterns that should be filtered out before delivery.

## Global Anti-Tells

- Do not use generic purple-blue AI SaaS gradients as a substitute for product hierarchy.
- Do not place every section inside a large card.
- Do not add an eyebrow label to every section.
- Do not mix table, card, and feed patterns for the same business list.
- Do not switch dark and light surfaces randomly mid-page.
- Do not use glow as a substitute for clear status, severity, focus, or hierarchy.

## Execution / Runtime Anti-Tells

- Do not turn execution pages into marketing heroes.
- Do not use decorative fake terminals when a real log stream or state table is needed.
- Do not hide retry, cancel, copy, or export actions away from the run context.
- Do not wrap every log row or timeline step in an oversized card.
- Do not dilute JSON, trace, or log data with decorative containers.

## Dashboard Anti-Tells

- Do not make operational dashboards overly spacious when users need scanning density.
- Do not turn unrelated metrics into identical cards without hierarchy.
- Do not use marketing copy where labels, state, and actions are needed.

## Component Anti-Tells

- Do not create one-off inputs, buttons, badges, or row styles for the same semantic role.
- Do not skip disabled, loading, error, focus-visible, or mobile states.
- Do not use text-only controls where a standard icon button is clearer.
